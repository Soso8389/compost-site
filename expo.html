CVHS Can Compost is the only student-run composting program in GUSD. Here is why you should be part of it.

01

Win a gift card every month

The top composter on the leaderboard wins a gift card at the end of every month. All you have to do is compost your lunch and log it through your account.

02

Earn real service hours

Volunteer at the compost station during lunch and every shift counts toward your community service requirement. Help your school sort their trash and make a difference at the same time.

03

Prepare for your future

Environmental leadership, community initiative, measurable impact, colleges love to see students who went beyond the classroom. Helping run a campus composting program checks all those boxes.
