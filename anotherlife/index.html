Trade your trash for a treat

Composting turns food scraps into rich soil, and that soil grows new food. It is a loop, and it usually takes months. Another Life speeds the loop up.

Stop by the compost cart with your leftover food waste and trade it in for a snack on the spot. Your scraps stay out of the landfill, and you get something back right now.

Why we are doing this

Most of the food waste at school ends up in the trash, where it breaks down without doing anyone any good. Compost does the opposite: it returns nutrients to the soil so new food can grow.

Food waste→ Compost→ Soil→ New food

Another Life shows that loop in action, only faster. Our goal is to make composting something students can see, touch and get rewarded for, so it becomes a habit and not a chore.

Every tray you bring to the cart is food kept out of the landfill. Thanks for being part of it.
